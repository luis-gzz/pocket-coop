# The HUD band sits between the garden and the toolbar

Supersedes ADR-0025's band sizing; the HUD keeps its fixed 13-pixel design at world scale. The HUD (coin and egg counters, shop and barn buttons) used to sit in a top band above the garden. It now sits in its own band right under the garden, above the toolbar, so every control is within a thumb's reach at the bottom of the screen. Layout now runs bottom up: the toolbar band is as tall as a slot and its margin, the HUD band sits on it, and the garden fills everything from the top edge down to the HUD band. That edge is where the old top HUD sat: a little below the screen's top, or most of the way down a notch or island's inset, so the hen can walk up beside the notch rather than stopping below it. Halfway down the inset put her into the notch. The alternative was to keep the garden at its old fixed height and leave the freed top space empty. We chose a taller garden, so the play area grows on tall phones. Taking an item back still only counts when it's dropped on the toolbar band, not the HUD band.

The shop and barn views put their X back at the top of the screen, in the barn button's old top spot, with the content below. The shop also moves the coin and egg counters up beside it; the barn view doesn't show them. We first kept the HUD band in place over the views, then moved it back to the top for full-screen pages. The shop's tabs stay at the bottom of the screen.
