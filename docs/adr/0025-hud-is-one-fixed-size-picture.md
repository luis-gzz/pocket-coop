# The HUD is one fixed-size picture, and the top band is sized to fit it

The barn button's height used to follow the top band, which ranged from 11 to 15 world pixels across devices, so its icon had to be scaled by a different factor than its frame (ADR-0024). Their pixels didn't match, and the icon sat tighter or looser from device to device. The HUD (barn button, egg counter, and the barn view's close button) is now one fixed design: a 13-pixel button holding a hand-drawn 9x9 barn, a pixel of cream, and the outline, drawn at world scale so its pixels match everything else. If a device can't fit 13 world pixels, the whole HUD scales down by a whole-number factor rather than squeezing the icon alone. The bands are sized around it: the top band is just tall enough for the HUD (a small gap below the screen's top or a notch, the button, and a pixel above the garden), the garden keeps its walkable height directly beneath, and the toolbar band gets everything left below. This supersedes ADR-0009's fixed split.
