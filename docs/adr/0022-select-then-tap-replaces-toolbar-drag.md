# Select-then-tap replaces dragging from the toolbar, and the selected item wins every world tap

Items used to be placed by holding a toolbar slot and dragging a true-size ghost into the play area. The long hold was slow and fiddly on a small band, and it competed with sideways scrolling. Now a tap on a slot **selects** its item, and while it's selected a tap anywhere in the play area uses it: it places food, a treat or a fixture, or refills the bowl tapped with the bucket. That tap never reaches what's beneath, so a chicken, egg, dropping or placed item can't open a tooltip or be collected while something is selected.

We chose this over letting world objects keep their taps. In that version, feeding near a chicken would open its tooltip instead, and the same tap would mean different things depending on where it landed. The cost is that the player must deselect before collecting eggs, and the true-size preview the drag gave is gone. Long-press drags of chickens and fixtures still work, and starting one deselects the item.

A selection lasts one successful use: every item deselects once it is placed or a bowl is refilled. A sticky selection would let stray taps litter the garden and silently eat the next tap meant for a chicken or egg. A tap that does nothing leaves the item selected, so the player can retry a miss. Opening the barn view or any dialog deselects too, so a stale selection never eats the first tap afterwards.
