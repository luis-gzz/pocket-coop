# Feed owns the food side of the world, mirroring Coop's egg split

ADR-0007 split egg laying in two: Gauges gates WHEN a hen lays, Coop decides WHERE the egg goes, since beds and eggs belong to the coop as a whole rather than to any one hen. Feeding needed the same split for the same reason - Gauges now decides WHETHER a chicken eats (the ceiling and chance rolls, ADR-0010), while a new sibling module, `src/feed.lua`, decides WHAT there is to eat: every placed seed patch, lettuce, and mealworm, none of which belong to any one chicken.

A food source holds a pool of fullness (its capacity) that eating draws down, rather than a timer - one unit of capacity is one point of satiety delivered, so it depletes exactly as fast as it's actually eaten and needs no shared world clock, leaving ADR-0003's per-Gauges `now` untouched. Three no-s worth recording, since each is easy to "fix" back without this context: food is never a dirty item (feeding a chicken is never a punishment via cleanliness), a food source is never exclusively claimed (any number of chickens may eat from - and deplete - the same one at once), and `Feed.hasFoodSource()` deliberately ignores treats, so a mealworm sitting out of a chicken's reach can never suppress foraging and starve it.
